# Jaali Gold card back: spec
Reference: text ("gilded Mughal jaali card back") + motif research: Salim Chishti tomb screen, Fatehpur Sikri
(MIT tiling search P60: p4m, 8-point star {8/2} + squares; Met "Art of the Islamic World" image 15). No pixel ref
(the sandbox has no curl), so the anchor is the house "wine" back. Kind: card-back.

## Palette
| role | hex | share | note |
|---|---|---|---|
| ground | #6e1822 -> #34080e | 60% | wine, radial, darker at edges (house wine family) |
| gold leaf | #fbd48a / #e2a94a / #8a5a1c | 25% | 3-stop vertical gradient, never flat |
| gold under-line | #2a0b06 | - | 0.5 unit shadow under gold lines (emboss) |
| hole tint | #4a0e15 | 10% | inside the stars, slightly darker = "pierced" |
| jewel | #1f6b5a | <2% | one emerald in the medallion |

## Structure
- 4-fold mirror symmetry (p4m) over the field; card itself mirror both axes.
- Field: lattice tile 11 units: an {8/2} star (R 4.4) joined tip-to-tip on the axes, small diamonds at the tile corners.
- Borders: outer gold 1.8 at inset 4.5 (rx 6), inner 0.7 at inset 9 (rx 4), a band of dark ground between them with dots.
- Centre: cusped mehrab arch, 36 wide x 50 tall, gold frame 2.5, dark inner, a rosette (8-point star) inside.
- Corners: quarter rosettes in the four field corners.

## Texture & finish
- Gold leaf faked with gradient + 0.4 highlight line on the medallion + dark under-line offset (0.5, 0.6).
- No filters, no grain (invisible at 54 px).

## What makes it premium
- Real gold (highlight + shadow), crisp even lattice rhythm, calm ground.
- One strong focal shape (the arch) that reads at 54 px; lattice reads as texture.

## Baazi translation
- Wine ground from the house back, gold toward --gold-hi/--gold-deep. Lattice line 0.9 so it survives as texture.
- No calligraphy or religious inscriptions (common on real jaali/mehrab): purely geometric. The mehrab is an
  architectural arch shape; keep it decorative-architectural, no mosque iconography.
